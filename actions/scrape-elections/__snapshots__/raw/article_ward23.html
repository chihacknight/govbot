Meet the 28-year-old lawyer running to represent the 23rd Ward

Leonardo Rojas-Banda, a 28-year-old attorney and community organizer, kicked off his campaign on Aug. 23 for Chicago's 23rd Ward. Supporters gathered during Hispanic Heritage Month, a citywide celebration, to hear the candidate speak.

Rojas-Banda was born and raised on the Southwest Side and chairs a local civic group. Rojas-Banda said his message is rooted in lived experience representing the ward.

Alderman Silvana Tabares pushed back against Rojas-Banda's criticism, saying she has delivered for the ward and intends to seek another term.
