# 2026 Legislative Session Calendar

Source: LegiScan 2026 Legislative Schedule, updated August 20, 2026
(`tamara-notes/session-dates/8-20-2026_Session_Calendar.pdf`).
Territories (PR, GU, MP, VI) are not covered by this LegiScan calendar at all — their
session dates come from each territory's own legislature site (see notes below and
`session-dates-comparison.md`).

**Status key:** `✅ In session` | `⏸️ Out of session` | `❓ Unknown`

Status column reflects **2026-09-29** (not the PDF's publish date) — convene/adjourn windows
were compared against today's date, and three states whose PDF entry alone didn't resolve
cleanly (AK, ND, NC — see below) were checked against a live source before being marked.
Red dates in the PDF = estimated by LegiScan, not necessarily final.
