HB 492 Engrossed                       2026 Regular Session                                     Spell

Abstract: Creates the Governor's Task Force on Impaired Driving to reduce impaired driving
      incidents and related fatalities.

Proposed law creates the Governor's Task Force on Impaired Driving.

Proposed law provides for the policy and purpose of the task force.

Proposed law provides for the establishment, membership, organization, domicile, staffing
responsibilities, procedures, powers, duties, functions, and funding of the task force.

Proposed law provides for the assistance and cooperation of other agencies with the task force in its
work as well as the availability of certain facilities for use by the task force.

Proposed law requires the attorney general to assist the task force and provide written opinions on
any subject requested by the legislative fiscal officer.

(Adds R.S. 49:219.1.1-219.1.4)

Summary of Amendments Adopted by House

   The Committee Amendments Proposed by House Committee on Judiciary to the original bill:

   1.      Add a representative from the American Automobile Association to the membership of
           the task force.

   2.      Add an additional at-large representative appointed by the governor.
