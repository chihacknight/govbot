SUMMARY OF BILL: Repeals obsolete statutory requirements for the Department of
Education (DOE), the State Board of Education (SBE), and the Tennessee Higher Education
Commission (THEC) to conduct and report analyses of literacy outcomes, literacy instruction, and
educator preparation program affordability.

FISCAL IMPACT:
                                   NOT SIGNIFICANT

     Assumptions:

     • Tennessee Code Annotated § 49-1-908 required:
           o The DOE to conduct a landscape analysis of literacy outcomes and instruction by
               December 31, 2021, including a joint analysis with THEC on the affordability of
               educator preparation providers, the results of which were due to the education
               committees of the General Assembly by March 1, 2022; and
           o The DOE, SBE, and THEC to submit a report to the above committees by July 1,
               2024, on the implementation of the Tennessee Literacy Success Act.
     • The DOE fulfilled the requirements by issuing the following reports:
           o A Landscape Analysis of Foundational Literacy Skills in Tennessee (Pre-K to Grade 5),
               published in April 2022;
           o The Education Preparation Provider Affordability Report, published in March 2022, in
               partnership with THEC; and
           o The Tennessee Literacy Success Act Implementation Report, submitted July of 2024, in
               collaboration with the SBE and THEC.
     • As the statutory requirements have already been fulfilled, deleting Tenn. Code Ann. § 49-1-
       908 will not result in any significant impact to the revenues or expenditures of the DOE,
       SBE, and THEC. Any fiscal impact is estimated to be not significant.

CERTIFICATION:
