SUMMARY:

           House Bill 6302 would amend provisions of the Revised School Code related to the display of
           the United States flag in classrooms.

           Currently, the code requires the board of a school district or intermediate school district (ISD)
           or the board of directors of a public school academy (PSA) to ensure that a United States flag
           is displayed in each classroom or other instructional site where students recite the Pledge of
           Allegiance. 1

           The bill would instead require the board or board of directors to ensure that a United States
           flag is displayed, hoisted, draped, or placed in a stand or post in a prominent location in each
           classroom or other instructional site where students recite the Pledge of Allegiance.

           MCL 380.1347

FISCAL IMPACT:

           House Bill 6302 would have no fiscal impact on the state or on local school districts, public
           school academies, or intermediate school districts.
