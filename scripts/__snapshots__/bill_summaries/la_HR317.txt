HR 317 Original                        2026 Regular Session                                  Bayham

Urges and requests that the Dept. of Transportation and Development (DOTD) create a diagnostic
review team to study and assess the potential risks of the increasing railroad activity on the Norfolk
Southern Railway originating at the Alabo Street Wharf and potential ways to prevent any
foreseeable harm to the community. Requires the DOTD submit a written report detailing its
findings to the House transportation committee before Jan. 1, 2027.
